How the rights are delivered

Presentations and discussions

The sponsor prepares product information for the included presentation and speaking slots. Topics, speakers, format and timing of Association program appearances are agreed in the sponsorship agreement.

Communication and visibility

The sponsor provides the approved logo, brochure or message required by its tier. The Association publishes or distributes them through the listed channels; member contact data remains with the Association. Preferential influencer rates provide access to agreed pricing; any influencer engagement and fees are arranged separately.

Delivery evidence

The agreement specifies how delivery will be documented, such as publication links, screenshots, copies of materials or activity records where available. Evidence format, responsibilities and dates are agreed before execution.

Scope, delivery and consent

The selected tier defines the sponsorship rights. Dates, formats, materials and documentation are confirmed in writing. Introductions require mutual interest and consent. Sponsorship does not guarantee sales, buyer numbers or audience engagement. A delegation member’s participation does not authorize a personal product endorsement, advertising appearance or private sponsor session; these require separate consent.

Additional service partnership

An agreed in-kind contribution, such as direct coverage of a real accommodation or professional interpretation cost, can be discussed separately. The contribution and any related rights are confirmed in writing.
