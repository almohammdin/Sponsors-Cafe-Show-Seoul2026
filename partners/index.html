Practical regional experience

Saudi Arabia, the Gulf and the wider Middle East

Saudi Arabia is the core focus of this partnership. The Association also brings experience across the Gulf and the wider Middle East, helping frame product discussions around practical operator needs in the region.

Exhibition experience

The Association has participated in exhibitions in Syria, Egypt and the UAE, building practical exposure to food and beverage businesses across the region.

Relationships with operators

Proposed collaboration

Before, during and after the visit

Select activities that match your products, objectives and availability. The scope, timing, channels and reporting are subject to agreement.

1

Before the visit

Align on product relevance and operator questions. Prepare concise Arabic explanatory content for publication through agreed Association channels.

2

During the Seoul visit

Hold a focused operator discussion or product demonstration at an agreed time. Facilitate voluntary introductions to participants who express interest.

3

After the visit

Agree on a follow-up session or concise report covering discussion themes, operator requirements and next steps, with consent for any attributed content.

Example collaboration

Coffee automation and operational technology

A supplier could demonstrate workflow, consistency and service requirements, then discuss how the solution may fit Saudi cafe operations. Follow-up can focus on questions raised by interested operators and information needed for their own assessment.

Existing hosted visits remain part of the visit program on their agreed terms. Any additional partnership activity is discussed separately.

Partnership contribution

Contribution and scope agreed together

A focused proposal connects your business objectives with the agreed activities and a clearly defined contribution.

Cash sponsorship

An agreed financial contribution linked to a written scope of collaboration, communication and follow-up.

Direct coverage of a delegation cost

Agreed coverage of a real program cost, such as accommodation or professional interpretation, with the cost and arrangements confirmed in advance.

Scope and consent

Activities and contribution are confirmed in a written agreement. Introductions are voluntary and require participant consent. The Association retains member contact data. Commercial outcomes depend on participant interest and each company?s independent assessment.
